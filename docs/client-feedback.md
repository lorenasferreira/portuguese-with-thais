# Client Feedback & Future Ideas

## Portuguese Conversation Club

### Client idea
Create a Portuguese conversation club focused on advanced learners who want regular speaking practice in a small group.

### Possible format
- Weekly online conversation session
- Around 40 minutes per session
- Small groups
- Possible group size:
  - 1 group of up to 10 people
  - or 2 groups of 5 people
- Each session based on a specific topic related to Brazil, culture or everyday life
- Focus on conversation and advanced speaking practice
- Not positioned as a traditional private lesson

### Possible pricing ideas
- Low-entry option: around $15 per session
- Monthly membership option: around $30/month

### Open questions
- Final group size
- Session duration
- Weekly or biweekly format
- Whether payment is per session or monthly
- Whether members book individually or have a fixed recurring group
- Whether there will be more than one level/group
- Whether the club includes materials before or after each session
- Whether there will be a private WhatsApp/Discord/community space

### Product consideration
This could become a separate offer from private lessons and may deserve its own section or page in a future version of the site.

### Status
Future idea — not part of current V1.
